About Me

James Fox is currently pursuing his masters degree in Computer Science at Villanova University and has a full tuition assistantship and has been a TA for multiple courses. While at Villanova he is involved in a graduate research project in robotic grid assembly algorithms. He has completed a back end development internship at IBM Poughkeepsie working on IBM zOS development this summer.

James graduated from Scranton in 2025 with a computer science and Mathematical Sciences major. He was a member of the MAGIS honors program in STEM and worked with Dr. Frissell on MSTID research. Under this project he attended multiple conferences and defended an undergraduate thesis. He also worked as a techcon in OSSS providing technical support for faculty and students with LMS software. He was treasurer for Scranton’s Gaming club and was instrumental, in rejuvenating the ACM student chapter and organized many student focused events and meetings.
